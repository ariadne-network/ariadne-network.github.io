Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Ariadne ("the app") is a proxy tool that routes network traffic according to the rules of a profile. We provide no proxy servers and operate none.

We collect no data

  • The app has no accounts and needs no sign-in.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal information, browsing history, traffic or device identifiers.
  • The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics, crash reporting or tracking components.
  • We have no servers that receive your data.

Data kept on your device

The following stays on your device, under your control, and is deleted with the app:

  • the profiles you install or import, and subscription links (which usually hold your account credentials with your provider; kept in the system keychain on iOS);
  • cached rule sets and external node lists;
  • connection records (Activity) and live traffic figures, shown on the device only;
  • traffic statistics (daily traffic by network type, direct or proxied, node, policy and site), kept for the last 90 days and clearable at any time;
  • logs, kept in the app's container for use on the device. The Mac app's "Export Diagnostics" runs only when you choose it, and removes host names, addresses and credentials first.
  • the modules and scripts you install, and the data scripts keep;
  • the certificate for HTTPS decryption: made on your device, its private key kept in the system keychain, never leaving the device;
  • HTTP capture (only after you turn Record on): requests and responses with their headers and part of their bodies, the last 300, clearable at any time; DNS lookups (only after you turn Keep Lookups on) are kept in memory and gone when you disconnect.

HTTPS decryption and scripts (optional)

HTTPS decryption, rewrites and scripts are Pro features, off by default. Once you turn on HTTPS decryption and trust the certificate the app made, HTTPS traffic to the hosts your profile and modules list is decrypted on your device, processed by the rewrites and scripts you installed, and encrypted again. All of this happens on your device; decrypted content is not sent to us or to anyone else. Scripts, which you install from their URLs, may go online themselves (to look up your exit IP address, for example) as their authors wrote them: install only scripts you trust.

Where the app connects

The app goes online only for what you ask of it:

  • the subscription links you add and the addresses your profiles refer to (such as rule sets and external node lists), to download and update profiles;
  • country IP lists for GEOIP rules: downloaded and updated from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) when your profile has GEOIP rules;
  • the addresses of the modules and scripts you install and the data they refer to, to download them and update them daily; and the addresses the scripts you install ask for themselves;
  • the proxy servers you configure: once connected, your device's traffic goes directly or through those servers according to your profile's rules. They are run by the providers you choose; we cannot see or record traffic through them;
  • encrypted DNS: so that network operators cannot read or alter domain lookups, the app looks up names over encrypted DNS (DoH) by default, built in with Alibaba Cloud Public DNS and Tencent DNSPod; your profile may name other services. A DNS service sees the names looked up, under its own privacy policy;
  • latency tests: the test addresses from your profile or settings (Google's www.gstatic.com when neither sets one);
  • network diagnostics: when you run them, Apple's connectivity check, the encrypted DNS service, and Cloudflare (to show your exit IP address and region).

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud sync in the app (a Pro feature, off by default), your profiles, subscription links, node choices and subscription usage are stored in your own iCloud and kept the same on the devices signed in to your Apple Account. Profile contents and subscription links are end-to-end encrypted, readable only on your devices; we cannot access anything in your iCloud. Turning sync off does not delete the data on your device.

Notifications

Subscription traffic and expiry alerts are local notifications created on the device, off until you turn them on in Settings.

Purchases

Pro and the trial are In-App Purchases with Apple. Apple handles payment; the app verifies purchases on the device (StoreKit) and sends nothing about them to us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and knowingly collects no one's information.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page is updated along with its effective date.
