What do I need?

A proxy or a subscription from a provider you choose. Ariadne Net runs no servers of its own: it sends each connection directly or through your provider's servers, as the rules of your profile say.

How do I add a node or a subscription?

In the Profiles tab, tap +. Import Nodes… takes share links and QR codes; Add HTTP / SOCKS5 Proxy… adds a proxy by hand; Install from URL… installs a subscription (Pro).

Why does iOS ask to add a VPN configuration?

The app routes traffic through a packet tunnel on your device, which iOS and iPadOS let apps set up only as a VPN configuration. Nothing goes to us.

What is free, and what is Pro?

Free: one node you add yourself, with every protocol, rules, encrypted DNS and network diagnostics. Pro, a one-time purchase with a free 14-day trial, adds subscriptions, profiles of any number of nodes, Activity, statistics, Auto Switch, HTTPS decryption, scripts, widgets and more. Family Sharing is supported.

I bought Pro on another device. How do I get it here?

Settings › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

How do I remove the HTTPS decryption certificate?

iOS Settings › General › VPN & Device Management › the Ariadne CA profile › Remove Profile. Turn HTTPS decryption off in the app first.

Something does not connect.

Run Advanced › Network Diagnostics: it tests the connection, DNS and your exit IP. If a subscription stopped working, update it in the Profiles tab; some providers serve it only for a few minutes after you enable fetching on their website.
